Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

MusicPlayerApp ("the app") is a personal Android music player, built and used by its developer only.

Google Drive access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It uses this access only to:

  • list the audio files and folders inside the one music folder you choose;
  • read those files to show their tags and cover art, to stream them, and to download them to your phone when you ask;
  • move files to your Drive trash, but only when you explicitly choose "Delete from Drive" in the app.

What data is stored, and where

  • Everything stays on your phone. That includes the list of your Drive music (titles, artists, albums, cover images), your downloaded songs, a temporary playback cache, and your settings.
  • The app has no server. It doesn't send your data, files or account information anywhere except directly to Google's own APIs.
  • The app has no analytics, ads or tracking, and it shares no data with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can sign out in the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its stored data from the phone. Songs saved to the phone's Music folder stay until you delete them.
